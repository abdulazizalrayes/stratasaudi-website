Common questions

Serious counterparties usually ask some version of these first.

Do you act as legal counsel?

No. Strata Saudi is not a law firm and does not act as licensed legal counsel. We provide engineering-led technical and commercial risk advisory designed to work alongside internal teams and external counsel where appropriate.

Can you work confidentially before a dispute fully forms?

Yes. The confidential enquiry flow is structured precisely for early-stage mandate screening, conflict posture review, and selective intake.

Are retainers available?

Yes, where recurring project stress, governance requirements, or portfolio exposure justify ongoing oversight rather than one-off review.

Do you take every enquiry?

No. Selectivity is part of the operating model. We screen for mandate fit, conflict posture, downside significance, and whether we can add genuine decision value.
