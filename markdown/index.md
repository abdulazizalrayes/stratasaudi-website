About Strata

## Bridging Engineering Realities with Contractual Strategy

Strata Risk Advisory was established to address a recurring gap in cross-border construction projects: dispute positions are often prepared without sufficient technical engineering grounding. With over 20 years of project and dispute exposure within the Saudi market, Strata provides structured contract risk analysis, pre-litigation engineering review, and independent technical advisory support to international companies operating under Saudi-governed contracts.

Our role is advisory. Independent. Discreet. We bridge the critical intersection where engineering execution realities meet contractual interpretation.

- Infrastructure and giga-project exposure
- Pre-litigation case involvement
- Commercial risk oversight
- Engineering narrative development
- Independent advisory capacity

Services

## Five Structured Mandates

Strata operates under clearly defined service mandates, each designed to address specific stages of contract risk, dispute lifecycle, and supply chain integrity.

01

### Pre-Contract Risk Review

Technical and commercial risk assessment of Saudi-governed contracts prior to execution, identifying exposure before commitment.

- Risk allocation structure analysis
- Variation clause exposure review
- Notice provision compliance mapping
- Payment and termination exposure assessment
- Enforcement practicality within local market dynamics

02

### Project Risk Oversight

Retained advisory providing ongoing review of live project documentation to preserve entitlement position and reduce dispute exposure.

- Notice compliance review
- Correspondence sufficiency audit
- Variation strategy alignment
- Delay and disruption early warning mapping
- Commercial risk flagging

03

### Pre-Litigation Technical Advisory

Structured engineering support prior to formal dispute proceedings, ensuring technical evidence aligns with counsel-led strategy and commercial decision-making.

- Chronology reconstruction
- Engineering narrative development
- Document sufficiency assessment
- Technical claim viability review
- Exposure range estimation

04

### Independent Technical Opinion

Confidential second-opinion advisory for boards, law firms, or international headquarters assessing exposure in Saudi projects.

- Exposure range opinion
- Risk classification
- Engineering sufficiency assessment
- Pre-escalation advisory

05

### Supply Chain & Vendor Risk Assessment

Independent due diligence and risk assessment of vendors, subcontractors, and supply chain partners prior to engagement or during project execution. We help foreign companies verify who they are working with before commitments are made.

- Financial capability verification
- Technical capacity and track record assessment
- Ongoing dispute exposure screening and public proceeding checks
- Delivery capability evaluation against project requirements
- Risk classification and recommendation

### The Technical Partner Law Firms Are Missing

Legal teams win cases with evidence. But in construction disputes, the most decisive evidence is technical — buried in engineering details that lawyers are not trained to interpret.

Strata works alongside international and local law firms as their technical counterpart: identifying engineering details that strengthen or undermine a case, interpreting drawings, specifications, and site records, reconstructing what actually happened on the ground, and translating complex technical realities into language that supports counsel-led strategy and commercial decisions.

We do not replace legal counsel. We provide the technical foundation they would not otherwise have.

Market Insight

## Why Foreign Contractors Misjudge Saudi Risk Exposure

Underestimation of procedural notice requirements that, when missed, forfeit substantial entitlements regardless of merit.

Overreliance on international legal interpretation without understanding local enforcement context and institutional behaviour.

Engineering documentation gaps that become critical weaknesses once disputes move beyond negotiation.

Misalignment between contract language and on-site realities, creating exposure that compounds over project lifecycle.

Reactive rather than preventive dispute strategy, leading to substantially higher cost of resolution.
